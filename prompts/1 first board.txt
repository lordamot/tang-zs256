The keyboard

Not a hang, a shift held for good.  The firmware's kbd_parse compared the USB report slot by slot; a HID keyboard packs its six slots, so when the first of two held keys goes up the second moves into slot 0 and the firmware sent the core "release B, press B" for a key that never moved.  keyboard.v counted the chord keys' shifts (cs_n, ss_n): a chord key - cursor, Backspace, Delete, and the punctuation , . - = ; ' / that go through Symbol Shift - pressed twice and released once left Caps or Symbol Shift down until reset, and from then on every letter came out as a capital or a symbol.  Fast typing with rollover does exactly this.

- mnano/usb_host.c: the reports are diffed as sets (a key in the old report and not the new one is a release, in the new and not the old a press); releases are sent with the OSD open as well, so a key held while F12 opened it does not stay down in the matrix.
- tang/src/zs256/keyboard.v: three bitmaps (plain, with Caps Shift, with Symbol Shift), a shift held while any bit of its map is set - idempotent to a repeated press and to a release without its press.

General Sound

Fetched psbhlw's gs-firmware sources (the guideline's source 4) into the scratchpad and read them.  The card's interrupt handler at 0038h, 37.5 kHz, only reads four bytes out of buffers at 6000h-63FFh (the reads are what load the DACs); the main loop's generator (ENGINE/SGEN) has to fill those buffers in time.  In simulation the card's Z80 managed 1.08 M fetches/s in its poll loop: every memory access went through the SDRAM's slot B and waited about four of its T-states, so the card ran at roughly half the real 12 MHz, the generator fell behind the interrupt, and the ISR played stale buffers - noise.  Two more things wrong against the card's schematic (in the same sources): the INT was a 48-clock pulse, 16 of the card's T-states, shorter than a waited instruction, so interrupts were missed - the card's is an RC pulse of some microseconds (DD17.6, C18 10n, R8 470); and a host write of the data register on the same clock as the card's read let the clear win, losing the flag.

- gs.v: the ROM (32 KB) and the fixed 16 KB at 4000-7FFF are BSRAM inside gs.v and answer within T2, no wait state; only the window's RAM pages (8000-FFFF, page 1 up) take slot B.  The ROM copy is filled by snooping the loader's bytes to 280000h in top.v (the SDRAM's copy stays, unread); the testbench preloads uut.gsound.rom alongside the SDRAM model.  INT is held until the acknowledge.  The flags are set-wins.
- Measured: the card's fetch rate in its ROM checksum loop is now 1.37 M fetches/s in a loop of 181 T - 14 MHz with no wait state.  The 3.5 s run: the card runs its checksum and its 15-page RAM test (about 2 s, as on the real card) and reaches its command loop (294 143 status polls); the host is unchanged (menu, TR-DOS, interrupts); SDRAM read-after-write clean.

General Sound confirmed on the board - recorded.  The keyboard had a second cause, and it was not the keyboard.

Reproduced in simulation with your disk: +TRDA=soft/ZPLAY50.TRD +SDFAST +KEYS=1f +TYPE_MS=3300 boots to the shell at about 10 s of machine time (15 minutes of wall); a Down arrow at 14 s did nothing, and the debug bytes said why - IFF1 = 0.  The shell's arrow, Enter and Space handlers all go through a HALT waiting for the frame interrupt (64C6h), and interrupts were dead: the acknowledge count stopped at 504 in every run, at 10.62 s, before any key.

The IFF1 trace (+IFFTRACE, new) gave the last acknowledge: PC 63BBh.  The shell runs IM 2 with I = 3Bh and takes its vector from the bus - FFh on a Scorpion, through the 48 ROM's 3BFF/3C00 to FFFF and a JR into FFF4.  An interrupt acknowledge is an I/O cycle with M1 and the PC on the address bus, and ports.v selected General Sound on A7..A0 = B3/BB without M1, ahead of the FFh the acknowledge should get.  So the interrupt that arrived with PC = 63BBh (the JP z after BIT 4,A in the key loop) read the card's status, 7Eh, as its vector, and the CPU went through 3B7Eh into nowhere with interrupts off.  The next key's HALT was the hang.  The SMUC's select lacked M1 as well.  On the card the decoder has /M1 in it (VD10 on its schematic); the SMUC's ИД7 too.

- tang/src/zs256/ports.v: every card's select is qualified by M1 (smuc_hit, gs_sel; the mouse already was); the acknowledge reads FFh and strobes nothing.
- Confirmed in simulation: Down moves the shell's bar to ZX-NEXT, a second Down, Space selects and the file loads and plays, interrupts taken every frame through 20 s.
- The chord fix from before stands on its own; it was real too.

Built: make bitstream passes the gate (logic 45%, BSRAM 41/46), lint clean; bin/tang.fs is 0.1.2, the firmware unchanged from 0.1.1.  Claims: built, linted, simulated, timed - not flashed. lets extend GS onboard ram to 2Mb

****

Done: General Sound has 2 MB, the card's maximum - its page register is six bits, so 63 window pages of 32 KB plus the fixed 16 KB.

- gs.v: page is six bits (port 0's bits 5:0), the window's word address {01, page, A14..A2} = SDRAM words 80000h-FFFFFh, all of bank 1.
- The ROM's SDRAM copy sat at A0000h, inside that range now, so it moves to 400000h (byte address): mnano/romload.h ROM_ADDR_GS, top.v's snoop (poke_a[23:15] = 80h), the testbench's preload.  The copy is still read by nobody - gs.v runs from its BSRAM - but the loader has to write somewhere, and the firmware and the bitstream have to agree: FLASH BOTH.  An old firmware with the new core puts the ROM into RAM page 16 and the card's BSRAM stays empty; a new firmware with an old core loses the ROM.
- The firmware's RAM test at reset covers four times as much and takes about 10 s of machine time before the card answers commands (the same order on a real 2 MB card; psbhlw's 1.05b patch makes it optional).  A player that talks to the card inside that window will find it busy - ZPLAY boots in about that time, so watch for it.

Simulated 12 s: the test finds all 63 pages (3Fh to the host), the command loop is reached, read-after-write on the SDRAM clean over the 3.7 million words touched.  Built: timing gate passed (logic 46%, BSRAM 41/46), lint clean, make fw builds; bin/ has both.  Claims: built, linted, simulated, timed - not flashed.  VERSION 0.1.3, CHANGELOG, progress.md, fpga.md (the memory map), mcu.md, platform.md, README, CLAUDE.md updated.
